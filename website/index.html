How it works

Research first. Then concepts. Then tests.

Most ad fatigue is an angle problem, not a design problem. The fix starts with listening to the people who already buy.

Customer research

Reviews, competitor reviews, Reddit, forums and comment sections. I pull the pains, objections, desired outcomes and exact phrases your buyers use.

Angles and concepts

Each insight becomes an angle, each angle a concept: hook, message and format, with the customer quote it came from attached.

Ready-to-test creative

Static ad copy and layout briefs plus UGC and video scripts, organised as a test plan so every launch tells you something.

About

Hi, I'm Carl.

I'm a research-led creative strategist and direct-response copywriter for DTC brands. My work starts where most creative skips: reading what customers write about the problem, the product and the alternatives, then building concepts from their words.

I came to creative strategy from consulting, where I learned to structure messy information into a clear recommendation. I bring the same rigour to ad research: every concept has a reason, and every reason has a source.

  • Focus: DTC brands advertising on Meta, especially health, wellness and personal care
  • Background: Strategy consulting internship at Roland Berger, Paris
  • Languages: English and German
