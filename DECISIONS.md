# Decisions (taken autonomously, Nov 4–5, 2026)

Each one is a default you can overturn.

1. **Command Center = this repo (`carl-calendar`).** The brief said it isn't in GitHub, but the commit history ("Command Center 2.1", Cue dispatch, MCP) shows it is. I extended it instead of building a second hub.
2. **Why the MCP connection was read-only:** the OAuth consent screen only offered the scopes the client *requested*. claude.ai only requests the required `mcp:read`, so `mcp:write` was never offered. Fix: the consent screen now always offers write (you can untick it). **Reconnect the connector once** to get write access.
3. **Routines get their own door:** `POST /api/engine/mcp` with an Engine key (`cce_…`, made in Today → ⚙ → Engine key). It uses the same tools as `/mcp` and needs no OAuth dance.
4. **Sending goes through the Gmail API, not SMTP.** Cloudflare Workers can't do SMTP with an app password reliably, and the Gmail API also gives thread-based reply detection and bounce search for free. You connect the Workspace sending inbox once (separate from your calendar Google account).
5. **Two locks on sending.** The env var `SENDING_ENABLED` (ships `"false"`) AND the in-app switch. The switch refuses to turn on until you've added the postal address, connected the inbox and approved at least one batch. AI tools can't flip it, and the AI tool to approve an item stays disabled (the existing "consequential" permission is off).
6. **Every send passes one tested gate (`checkSendable`):** approved, payload unchanged since approval (hash), US/UK/CA/AU only, `email_source` is apollo/website/public_page (no guessed emails), not suppressed, no `{{placeholders}}`, under the warm-up cap (15 → +5/week → 40), 08–17 recipient-local on weekdays, random 7–19 min spacing, and not auto-paused (≥3 bounces in 24 h, >5% bounce rate in 7 days, or any complaint).
7. **Follow-up drafts contain placeholders** (`{{NEW_OBSERVATION}}`, `{{STATIC_OR_HOOK_CONCEPT}}`). The system won't invent observations. The morning routine fills them; until then they can't be approved or sent. The day-13 close is complete as written.
8. **New data lives in clean SQL tables** (`engine_records`, `outreach_items`, `outreach_sequences`, `suppression`, `send_log`, `job_runs`, `engine_activity`) rather than the client-synced `records` blob. Engine clients are mirrored into the old Clients screen, so there's still one hub.
9. **The business money tracker is separate from your personal money planner.** The old Money module stays personal. Business income, expenses, MRR, tax set-aside (a % you set, estimate only) and € per hour live in the Engine.
10. **Routing:** `#/` and `#/today` open Scriptroom Today, `#/today/setup` opens health and settings, and `#/engine` opens backend data with undo. All old screens are unchanged under `#/engine/<old path>`, and old links still work. Nothing was deleted. The legacy smoke tests (`tests/smoke.mjs`, `v3`, `v4`) assumed the old home screen at `/`. I didn't run them; they need `#/engine/home` as their start URL.
11. **Cron moved from every 15 min to every 5 min.** The legacy notifications still run every 15 min; the Engine ticks every 5.
12. **The Audit Engine and acquisition run as Claude scheduled routines, not inside the Worker.** Adlicio, Apollo and Upwork are connectors on your Claude account. The Worker does the deterministic parts (batch, sending, replies, health); Claude does the thinking parts and reports with `job_heartbeat`. Dead routines show red on Today.
