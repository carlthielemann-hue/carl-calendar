# Outreach Playbook v1 — The Profit Script
The daily acquisition run follows this document. It finds opportunities, researches them, and writes drafts for Carl's approval. Nothing is ever sent, submitted, posted or applied for without Carl's explicit approval. Drafts only.
## 1. Who Carl is (what we sell)
- Carl Thielemann, The Profit Script (theprofitscript.com). DTC creative strategist and direct-response copywriter.
- Core work: customer and market research, angles, hooks, Meta ad concepts (statics and UGC/video scripts), creative briefs, advertorials and presells, landing pages, testing plans.
- Edge: research-led concepts, fast end-to-end static production, bilingual EN/DE, consulting background (Roland Berger internship, Paris).
- Direction: from execution to creative strategy; paid for concepts and outcomes, not hours. Goal: €10k/month via 3–4 retainers.
- Market: English-speaking only.
## 2. Proof rules (hard)
- Never invent results, clients, numbers, testimonials or brand names.
- Allowed reference: "I've built static and script concepts for DTC brands in health/wellness and personal care" plus theprofitscript.com.
- Do not name clients (Keeps, Zoyava, Stepz) until Carl confirms. Say e.g. "a US men's hair-loss telehealth brand".
- No performance claims (ROAS, CTR, "scaled to X").
## 3. Ideal client profile
- Market: US, UK, Canada, Australia. No German/EU cold email.
- DTC / e-commerce brand selling its own product and running Meta ads. Also DTC-focused agencies hiring creative strategists.
- Signals: 10+ active Meta ads, own store, reviews, clear product.
- Best categories: supplements/wellness, skincare/beauty, personal care/grooming, pet, health devices, home goods.
- Avoid: dropshipping, MLM, adult, gambling, crypto, "get rich" offers, unverified Upwork payment, under $25/h or under $150 one-off (unless a clear foot-in-the-door with a strong brand).
## 4. Scoring
Criteria (Command Center ids): service_fit (weight 3), dtc_fit (2), demand (2), budget (2), maturity, timing, decision_maker, source_quality, deal_value (1 each). Score 0–5 with a one-line evidence-based reason. Only draft when service_fit ≥ 4 and dtc_fit ≥ 3.
## 5. Channels and daily caps
Caps v1: Upwork up to 5 drafts · job posts (X/LinkedIn) up to 3 · cold email up to 5 brands.
### 5a. Upwork
- Tool: Upwork find_jobs, org_uid 2021993558031787224.
- smart_search mode=most_recent, days_posted=1; plus search with title terms: "creative strategist", "ad creative", "UGC script", "static ads", "ad copywriter", "advertorial". sort=recency, verified_payment_only=true, proposals_max=20.
- Skip applied=true, non-English, non-DTC, low budget, duplicates.
- For each good fit: get the job (client_record, connects_cost), score it, write a proposal draft.
### 5b. Job posts on X and LinkedIn
- Tool: web search. Queries like "hiring creative strategist DTC", "looking for ad creative strategist", "UGC scriptwriter hiring", "static ad designer hiring", restricted to x.com and linkedin.com. Prefer the last 7 days.
- Only real hiring posts by a brand, founder or agency. Draft a short reply or DM.
### 5c. Cold email ("5-minute ad audit")
- Find brands: Adlicio find_competitor_brands for 1–2 ICP niches per day (rotate supplements, skincare, grooming, pet, wellness devices). Pick 5 that match the ICP.
- Research: Adlicio find_competitor_ads (live Meta ads) and inspect_landing_page.
- Audit: 2–3 specific observations about visible ads only (angle variety, hook strength, missing formats, offer clarity, ad-to-page match) plus one concrete test idea. Never claim to know their performance or spend.
- Contact: founder / head of growth / marketing lead, with an email verified from a real source. Never guess an email. If none: mark "find contact".
- Draft the email (template below), follow-up date +4 days.
## 6. Voice
- Short, specific, peer-to-peer. Sharp operator, not an agency. Slightly imperfect over polished.
- No buzzwords (synergy, game-changer, unlock, elevate), no hype, no emojis in email, no "I hope this finds you well".
- Lead with something specific about their ads or post. One idea, one ask. English only. Sign "Carl" (plus "The Profit Script" on first touch).
## 7. Templates
### Upwork proposal (120–180 words)
- One line showing you read the post (their product and goal in your words).
- How you'd approach it: research, then 2–3 specific angles you'd test first.
- Relevant experience (section 2 only) plus theprofitscript.com.
- One clarifying question that shows judgment.
- Easy next step ("Happy to send 3 hook ideas before we start").
### Cold email, first touch (80–110 words, two variants)
Subject: 3–5 plain words about their ads.
A (audit in email): Was looking at your Meta ads for [product]. Two things stood out: [observation 1], [observation 2]. One test I'd run: [idea]. Want me to send 3 concepts for it?
B (teaser): Did a quick 5-minute teardown of your current Meta ads and found 2 angles you're not testing yet. Want me to send it over?
Footer: Carl · The Profit Script · theprofitscript.com — "Not relevant? Just reply 'no' and I won't follow up."
### Follow-ups
- Day 4: one new observation or angle, 2–3 lines.
- Day 8: a quick static or hook concept for their product, 2 lines.
- Day 13: friendly close: "I'll leave it here, happy to share the ideas if timing's better later."
- Any reply stops the sequence.
## 8. Never
- Never send, submit, apply, post, DM or email anything. Drafts and approval only.
- Never cold-email EU/German brands. Never guess emails or budgets.
- Never draft for a job where applied=true or a lead that already has a pending draft.
## 9. Where output goes
Preferred: Command Center (submit_opportunity + submit_outreach_draft into Carl's approval queue). If Command Center is read-only, append the run to the Google Doc "The Profit Script – Outreach Inbox" instead, newest run on top.
## 10. End of run
Short run report: found, drafted, skipped (with reasons), decisions Carl needs to make. Under 10 lines.
